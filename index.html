Privacy Policy

MadhuPriyan — Nearby nearby Shops & Product Stock Information

Effective date: 18 November 2026 Last updated: 18 November 2026 App: MadhuPriyan

This Privacy Policy explains how MadhuPriyan ("the App"), developed by Sanoj Lawrence ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information when you use the App. MadhuPriyan helps users find nearby nearby shops, view shop information on a map and check product stock information.

Location disclosure: When you use the nearby-shop feature, the App obtains your device location (if you grant permission) and sends the latitude and longitude to the external shop information service at MadhuPriyan.api to retrieve nearby shop information. Location is therefore transmitted outside your device for this feature.

1. Information We Use

Device location: With your permission, the App accesses approximate or precise location through Android location services while you use the App. Location is used to calculate nearby shops, request shop information relevant to your position and display shop locations on the map. You can deny or revoke location permission in Android settings; nearby results may then be unavailable or less useful.

Search and shop requests: When you request nearby shops or shop/product information, the App sends the information needed for that request, such as your latitude and longitude for nearby-shop searches or a shop number for shop-specific requests, to the relevant API endpoint. The API returns shop and product information for display in the App.

Advertising and device information: The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Next-Gen) to display advertisements. Google's SDK disclosure says the SDK may automatically collect and share information such as IP address, app interactions (for example, app launches, taps and ad/video views), diagnostic information and device or account identifiers (including advertising ID and app set ID). Google may use this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The exact data processed can depend on SDK version, configuration and available ad features.

Map requests: The App uses MapLibre to display maps and obtains map tiles from OpenFreeMap. When map tiles are requested, the map service may receive technical request information such as your IP address, device/browser information and the map tiles requested. OpenFreeMap describes its logging practices in its Privacy Policy .

2. How We Use Information

  • To identify and display nearby shops near your current location.
  • To show shop locations and map information.
  • To retrieve shop details and product stock information when requested.
  • To display advertisements and support advertising measurement, analytics and fraud prevention through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot and improve the App and its features.

3. How Information Is Shared

We do not operate the nearby API or the OpenFreeMap map tile service. Information is sent to these services when required for the feature you use:

  • nearby API: Your latitude and longitude are sent to https://shopdashboard-api.external-service.example/ when you request nearby shops. Shop numbers and other request parameters may be sent when you request shop-specific information. The API operator processes these requests and returns shop or product information. Its handling and retention of request data are governed by the service operator's practices.
  • Google Mobile Ads: The advertising SDK may collect and share the information described above with Google and relevant advertising/measurement partners to provide and measure ads, prevent fraud and support SDK functionality. See Google's Next-Gen SDK data disclosure .
  • OpenFreeMap: Map tile requests are sent to the map service so that the map can be displayed. See OpenFreeMap's Privacy Policy .

We do not use your precise location for the purpose of serving advertisements. Advertising SDKs may independently use IP address or other permitted signals to estimate general location, as described in Google's documentation.

4. Location Permission and Your Choices

Location permission is requested to provide nearby-shop functionality. The App does not need background location access for its normal nearby-shop feature. You can manage or revoke location permission at any time from your Android device's Settings > Apps > MadhuPriyan > Permissions. If permission is denied, you can continue using features that do not require your location, but nearby results may not work as intended.

5. Data Security

Requests to the nearby API use HTTPS. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. We take reasonable steps within the App to avoid exposing information unnecessarily. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

The App uses location to fulfil the nearby-shop request and display relevant results. We do not specify the retention period of data processed by external services because those services are operated independently of us. Please refer to the relevant service provider's privacy information for its retention practices. Google and OpenFreeMap publish information about their respective data handling practices through the links above.

7. Children's Privacy

MadhuPriyan is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. The App provides information about shops and products and is not intended to encourage unlawful access to age-restricted products. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us using the details below.

8. Third-Party Services and Links

The App depends on services operated by third parties, including the nearby API, Google Mobile Ads and OpenFreeMap. These services have their own privacy policies and data practices. We do not control the independent privacy practices of these providers. We encourage you to review their policies.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, services or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published at the same location with a revised "Last updated" date. Please review this page periodically.
