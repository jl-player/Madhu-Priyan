Privacy Policy

Madhu Priyan

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Madhu Priyan ("we", "our", or "the app") is an Android application designed to help users locate nearby TASMAC shops and browse available shop or product information. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how information may be used, and how third-party services handle data.

1. Information We Access

Location Information

Madhu Priyan may request access to your device's location to display your current position on a map and help you find nearby shops.

  • Location access supports location-based features.
  • Your location coordinates are used locally on your device to display your position and determine nearby shops.
  • We do not intentionally transmit your precise GPS coordinates to our own servers or third parties.
  • You can deny or revoke location permission through your Android device settings. Location-based features may not work without permission.

Advertising and Device Information

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process certain information, which may include:

  • Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers.
  • IP addresses and general location information derived from them.
  • Advertising interactions and app usage information.
  • Diagnostic information and SDK performance data.

This information may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, subject to Google's applicable policies and settings.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed or processed through the app may be used to:

  • Display your current location and nearby shops.
  • Display shop and product information.
  • Load map tiles, images, and other app resources.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Maintain app functionality, security, and reliability.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. We do not intentionally transmit your precise GPS coordinates to our own servers or third parties. However, third-party services integrated into the app, including advertising and map-resource providers, may process technical information as described in this policy.

Information handled by third-party services is subject to their respective privacy policies and practices.

5. Data Storage and Security

The app uses device location for local map functionality rather than intentionally transmitting precise GPS coordinates to our own servers.

Network requests to remote resources may expose technical information to the relevant service providers. We use secure connections where supported to help protect information transmitted over the internet.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a separate server-side database of users' precise GPS coordinates as part of the app's local location feature.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to their respective privacy policies and retention practices.

You can revoke location permission at any time through Android settings and manage available advertising privacy settings through your device and Google's services.

7. Children's Privacy

Madhu Priyan is not intended to encourage the use of alcohol or to promote the purchase or consumption of alcoholic beverages by minors. The app is intended for users who are legally permitted to access its relevant services in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information directly. If you believe personal information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us.

8. Your Choices

You can:

  • Deny or revoke location permission through Android settings.
  • Manage available advertising privacy settings on your device.
  • Stop using the app or uninstall it at any time.

Some app features may be unavailable if required permissions are denied.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app functionality, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. Any revised policy will be published at the same public URL, with the effective date updated accordingly.
